About

slashslash games is an independent game development project, currently entirely run by me(!), Declan Carveth.

My first game under slashslash is Quantum Loop, which released on Steam on the 26th of November 2025.

I also use the label slashslash records as an imprint for music I independently distribute - my current main music project is Apryl.
